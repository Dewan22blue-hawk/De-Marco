# Product Vision & PRD (Product Requirements Document)

## Nama Produk
**Deraly AI** (Project: De-marco)

## Big Vision
The AI Operating System for Marketing Communication & Business Development atau AI Business Growth Platform.

Platform ini bukan sekadar AI Writer atau Content Editing Studio (seperti Canva), melainkan **AI Business Operating System (AI Business OS)** khusus untuk:
- Aktivitas pemasaran (Marketing)
- Periklanan (Advertising)
- Bisnis Konten (Content Business)
- Komunikasi Bisnis (Corporate Communication)
- Pengembangan Kemitraan & Business Development

## Core Value
Bukan hanya membuat tulisan, tetapi membantu perusahaan menjalankan aktivitas Business Development secara *end-to-end* dalam satu dashboard AI terpusat dan terintegrasi. 

**Contoh Use Case:**
Menawarkan Sistem Smart City kepada Pemerintah Kabupaten Bandung.
AI akan langsung membuat (menggunakan aset standar Deraly seperti logo, color palette, dll):
- Surat Penawaran
- Executive Summary
- Proposal
- Company Profile
- Poster & Banner
- Social Media Post
- Presentasi & Timeline
- Quotation & Draft MOU

---

## Fitur Utama (MVP / Minimum Viable Product)

Aplikasi dibagi menjadi 6 AI Engine utama:

### 1. AI Marketing Content Studio
Generator konten dengan aset Deraly (background, template). AI memahami target audience, tone, objective, dan CTA.
- Instagram Post, LinkedIn, Facebook, X
- Website Copy, Landing Page
- Email Marketing, WhatsApp Broadcast
- Caption, Blog, Press Release, Newsletter, Company News

### 2. AI Business Development Assistant
Generator presentasi dan deck untuk kebutuhan marketing/BD.
- Proposal, Company Profile, Product Deck, Sales Deck
- Executive Summary, Business Plan, Pitch Deck
- Partnership / Sponsorship Proposal

### 3. AI Corporate Document Generator
(Killer Feature) Generator dokumen resmi dengan standar Deraly (cover letter, kop surat, tanda tangan).
- **Surat**: Penawaran, Kerja Sama, Permohonan, Undangan, Balasan
- **Legal**: NDA, MOU, MoA, PKS, Kontrak
- **Dokumen**: TOR, RAB, Invoice, Quotation, Purchase Order, BAST Project

### 4. AI Visual Content Studio
Generator aset visual (otomatis memilih tema: Background, Illustration, Icons, Gradient, Typography, Layout).
- Poster, Flyer, Banner, Feed IG, Carousel, Story, Thumbnail, Roll Up Banner, Billboard.
- **Style Themes**: Modern, Corporate, Luxury, Healthcare, Education, Government, Technology, Minimal, Claymorphism, Glassmorphism, Neumorphism.

### 5. AI Brand Asset Manager
Database untuk menyimpan identitas perusahaan. AI akan secara otomatis memakai aset ini.
- Logo, Icons, Illustration, Company Color, Font, Background, Pattern, Brand Guideline.

### 6. AI Campaign Planner
Generator strategi kampanye lengkap.
- Marketing Calendar, Campaign Timeline, Promotion, Content Schedule, Hashtag, CTA, Persona, KPI, Budget.

---

## Killer Features
1. **AI Business Copilot**: Prompt satu kalimat untuk men-generate keseluruhan workflow (Analisis, Proposal, Surat Penawaran, dll).
2. **AI Brand Brain**: Menyimpan *knowledge* perusahaan (Visi, Misi, Brand Voice, Layanan, Portfolio) agar output AI selalu konsisten.
3. **AI Prompt Library & Template Marketplace**: Menyediakan berbagai kategori prompt dan template untuk berbagai industri.

## Roadmap Pengembangan

| **Phase 3** | AI Campaign & CRM | Kalender kampanye, pipeline prospek, integrasi email dan WhatsApp Business |
| **Phase 4** | AI Business Intelligence | Analitik kampanye, scoring prospek, rekomendasi strategi, dan dashboard KPI |
